The Framework Inside

Claim. Prime.
Build. Reflect.

Four steps. Any schedule. You don't need to do all four perfectly — you need to do something every day.

Claim Protect your window before the world claims it.
Prime Move your body before excuses settle in.
Build One meaningful thing that moves life forward.
Reflect Review honestly, adjust, and repeat.

This plan works across every schedule type — early riser, night shift, new parent, student, trade worker. The 4AM adjustments section covers all of them.
