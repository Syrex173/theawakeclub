The Protocol

How to Use
This Page.

The drift happens in the search. Open a tab, start scrolling for the right playlist, and the session starts before the music does.

Eliminate the search. Four steps. Every session.

01

Pick one category.

Match the energy to the session you planned. Not the mood you're in right now.

02

Open one source.

Spotify or YouTube. Not both. Not three tabs.

03

Put your phone down.

Click. Lift. Don't Drift. The music does not need you to manage it.

04

Train without drifting.

Put your mind where your weight is. Every set. Every rep.
